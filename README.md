# carekind-blog

Articles for the **CareKind AI** blog — Australian aged care news and what it means for providers and care teams.

- `drafts/` — AI-assisted drafts written each morning (08:00 Sydney). **Not reviewed or endorsed yet** (`status: draft-pending-review`).
- `posts/` — articles reviewed and approved by a person.
- Writing rules, review checklist and all other documentation live in the meta repo: [blog-editorial-guide.md](https://github.com/terryxu0321/JRAgedCareProject/blob/main/company-os/07-marketing-operations/blog-editorial-guide.md).

Articles summarise public information and are not legal or clinical advice.
